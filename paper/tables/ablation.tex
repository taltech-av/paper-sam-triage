\caption{Annotation variant ablation on CLFTv2-Base (RGB--LiDAR CrossFusion).
         The ladder is nested: each step adds exactly one pipeline stage to the one above it, so a
         change in score is attributable to that stage and to nothing else.
         All variants train on the same 2{,}642 frames --- the flagged frames that carry no human
         labels --- and are evaluated on the same held-out 1{,}001 frames, which are exactly the
         frames a human verified, against those human labels.
         No human label is therefore spent on training, and the evaluation reference is independent
         of every variant's own training labels: scoring a model against the pseudo-labels it was
         trained on measures label self-consistency rather than segmentation quality, and inflates
         precisely the variants with the most idiosyncratic labels.
         mIoU averages vehicle, sign, and human (cyclist + pedestrian combined) and is the mean over
         the five weather-condition mIoUs; fw-IoU weights each class by its pixel frequency.
         The last column reports the \emph{paired} difference in mIoU against the Swin only variant,
         with a 95\% CI from a bootstrap over test frames (10{,}000 resamples, stratified by weather
         condition; the same resample indices are applied to every variant).
         A paired difference is the statistic this design supports: all variants are evaluated on the
         same frames, so per-variant marginal CIs overlap far more than the differences themselves and
         understate the separation.
         An interval excluding zero means the variant differs significantly from Swin only.
         The three VLM-independent rows are final; the four VLM rows are \pending{pending} on runs
         still in progress and no value is reported for them in advance.}
